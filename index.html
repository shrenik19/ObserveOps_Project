ObserveOps

Reference screens

Two product screens rebuilt entirely from the published @mtdt/observeops-ds-* packages, as an outside consumer would. No hardcoded colours, no invented components. The design-system gap report these screens produced is in docs/DS-GAPS.md.

Report module

Category-level Public/Private visibility and sharing. Paired padlock indicators, a hover-only custom-category marker, and a four-step delete flow that reassigns or force-deletes the reports inside a category.
